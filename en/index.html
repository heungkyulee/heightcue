The four-step measurement setup

Use a hard floor and vertical wall.

Keep the setup consistent. Avoid carpet and thick baseboards.

Remove shoes.

Keep both heels flat. Stand straight and relaxed.

Keep the eyes level.

Face forward. Keep the chin relaxed.

Record the date and result.

Measure at roughly the same time. Follow the trend across repeated records.
