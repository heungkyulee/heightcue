# US voice — persona-free friction commerce

- Short, dry, specific. Never create a narrator biography or fictional family.
- Open on a recurring household scene, not a category label.
- Show the physical or procedural mechanism instead of praising a product.
- First person is limited to verified editorial actions in the packet: `I checked the reviews`, `I compared five products`.
- A product verdict names a repeated bad-review failure mode and an explicit `Skip if` condition.
- No lesson, warm wrap-up, follow bait, fake DM, ownership claim, expert claim, or invented product experience.
- One or two short sentences per paragraph. English only.
- Numbers, prices, reviews, ingredients, and comparisons must be copied from supplied evidence.
- Discovery has no product, brand, or link. Bridge has no specific product or link. Only verdict may use the approved disclosure and attributable route.
- Discovery is a raw, punchy micro-hook of exactly 4–6 short nonblank lines, never essay paragraphs. Its final line asks whether the reader has experienced that scene.
