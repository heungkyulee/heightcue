# HeightCue SSOT v3 — Persona-Free Friction Commerce

**Effective:** 2026-08-31
**Archive:** `docs/archive/heightcue-SSOT-v2-persona-era.md` preserves the superseded identity-era document and is not an active prompt source.

## 0. Non-negotiables
1. No narrator biography or replacement demographic persona. Historical identity material is archive-only.
2. Never fabricate product use, ownership, family, DM, expert status, reviews, prices, evidence, or outcomes.
3. Preserve required affiliate disclosure, medical/evidence limits, product fidelity, approval, attribution, and publication read-back gates.
4. Verified commission and orders are the north star. Views are reach evidence only.

## 1. Business and audience
HeightCue serves parents and caregivers by finding recurring friction in households with children and showing the simplest low-consideration product mechanism that removes it. It does not expand to generic household novelty gadgets.

Eligible domains include mess/cleaning, storage/space, schoolwork/desk ergonomics, meal and feeding logistics, sleep environment, bathing/dressing, travel/outings, safe household participation, noise/indoor play, routines/organization, and evidence-gated nutrition convenience.

## 2. Low-consideration eligibility
A candidate must make the problem and mechanism understandable on one mobile screen; require no professional advice, fitting, or long comparison; have limited reversible downside; sell through convenience/time/mess/noise/space/durability/argument reduction rather than health outcomes; and have repeated review evidence for friction and failure modes. High-risk safety, diagnosis/fitting, height-growth promises, creator-testimony dependence, and unsupported health claims fail closed.

Price is a market-specific component, not a universal hard threshold.

## 3. Editorial operating identity
The only stable identity is the selection rule. Voice is short, dry, and specific. Start with a recognizable sourced scene, identify the physical/procedural friction, show mechanism, check bad reviews, and state a concrete skip condition. First person is limited to packet-recorded editorial actions such as checking reviews, labels, or comparisons.

## 4. Three content stages
### discovery
One repeated household moment and its cost in time, mess, noise, space, waste, or arguments. No product, brand, affiliate link, disguised recommendation, or forced solution.

### bridge
One generic form-factor mechanism in one screen. No specific commercial product, brand, affiliate link, or direct coupling to an ad.

### verdict
Required order: friction-first micro-hook; approved market disclosure; mechanism; one or two verified selection facts; repeated bad-review failure mode and why the winner reduces it; explicit `skip if`; attributable route.

All candidates carry `friction_id`, `stage`, `market`, and `source_pointers`; factual claims also carry `evidence_pointers`. Non-commercial stages may share `friction_id` with a verdict but may not directly link or reply-chain into it.

## 5. Friction ledger
Each record stores source type and pointer, verbatim complaint, market, recurrence, intensity, associated mechanisms/form factors, and lifecycle. Allowed origins are validated internal engagement, attributable external caregiver complaint, repeated marketplace review friction, or measured prior conversion. Category inventory rotation alone is not demand.

## 6. Inspectable candidate scoring
Store each 0–5 component and source pointers separately: `friction_frequency`, `friction_intensity`, `mechanism_clarity`, `mobile_demo_clarity`, `consideration_cost`, `price_resistance`, `review_evidence_strength`, `failure_mode_severity`, `compliance_cost`, `expected_commission_value`, and `attribution_readiness`. Eligibility gates are separate from the final score and always fail closed.

## 7. Revenue learning hierarchy
Rank observed outcomes lexicographically:
1. verified commission and orders
2. verified outbound affiliate clicks
3. guide/profile progression tied to `friction_id`
4. qualified engagement on the matching friction
5. views

A high-view pattern cannot be called a revenue winner without higher-level evidence. Reports separate observed metrics from strategic hypotheses.

## 8. Prompt and archive boundary
Active prompt assembly loads the user-intent contract, compliance, persona-key compatibility editorial context, market voice, and named skill only. Narrative archives are never loaded. Identity-era posts, ledgers, designs, and analytics remain unchanged as history.

## 9. Analytics and account memory
Track `friction_id`, stage, mechanism, product, price band, hook family, format, affiliate destination, clicks, orders, and commission. Account memory rotates topics and identifies funnel bottlenecks from observed data; it never invents a daily personality or narrative cadence.

## 10. Acceptance gate
Active prompts and code contain no retired narrator requirements; source and score components serialize intact; stage separation fails closed; verdict requirements are validated; both markets retain their exact approved disclosures and attribution routes; all changed behavior has red-green regression coverage.
