01 · Does it clean your rugs?

The Tineco FLOOR ONE S5 is a hard-floor washer. Tineco says it cannot clean carpet. If you need one machine for both your rugs and hard floors, that rules this model out.

Tineco’s S5 FAQ. Other Tineco models have different uses; check the exact model.

02 · What does “up to 50 minutes” mean?

For the Shark PowerPro Pet IZ373H, Shark measures its 50-minute claim at the hand vacuum in standard mode. That isn’t a promise of 50 minutes cleaning floors with the floorhead attached.

When comparing cordless models, look for the attachment and power setting beside the runtime claim.

03 · Will you carry it upstairs?

The Shark ZU102 is a corded upright weighing 15.19 lb according to Shark. It is listed for carpets and floors. If cordless use or a light machine is essential, keep looking.
