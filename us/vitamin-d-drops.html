What the evidence actually supports

NORMAL ROLE

NIH explains that vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium from food.

DEFICIENCY COST

NIH notes that children who do not get enough vitamin D can develop rickets, with soft and weak bones.

HEIGHT LIMIT

In 8,851 school-age children with low baseline vitamin D status, supplementation increased vitamin D levels but did not affect growth over three years.
